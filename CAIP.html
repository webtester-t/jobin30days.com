JOB IN 30 DAYS™

Career Development & Program Promotion Intern

Learn. Earn. Grow. Help Others Succeed.

JOB IN 30 DAYS™ is a career mentorship platform dedicated to helping job seekers significantly increase their chances of landing roles that match their potential. It offers tailored services such as CV reviews, LinkedIn profile optimization, Job search strategy, and interview preparation to strengthen professional and interpersonal skills. It also publishes books and resources that provide practical insights to stay ahead in a fast-changing job market.

Whether individuals are refining their skills or preparing for critical interviews, JOB IN 30 DAYS™ delivers tools and guidance to support career success. Learn more about its services and resources at Jobin30Days.com.

We are looking for enthusiastic and self-driven interns who are passionate about career development, digital marketing, sales, content development, and helping others succeed.

Internship Mode

  • Remote (Work from Home)

Duration & Type

  • 3–6 months (Flexible)
  • Type: Internship

Roles & Responsibilities

  • Promote JOB IN 30 DAYS™ mentorship programs through online and offline channels.
  • Develop Content (Text, Video, Audio) for social media, blogs, and articles.
  • Generate leads and connect with students, fresh graduates, and job seekers.
  • Explain the benefits of our books, courses, and mentorship programs.
  • Assist in social media promotion and community engagement.
  • Build relationships with potential learners and guide them through the enrollment process.
  • Maintain records of leads and follow-ups.
  • Contribute ideas to increase awareness and program registrations.

Who Can Apply?

  • Students pursuing Graduation or Post-Graduation
  • Fresh graduates
  • Individuals interested in content development, sales, marketing, HR, recruitment, or career counselling
  • Good communication skills in English and/or Hindi
  • Self-motivated individuals willing to learn and take initiative

What You'll Learn

  • Job Readiness and Employability Skills
  • Professional Communication
  • Resume and LinkedIn Best Practices
  • Career Planning and Job Search Strategies
  • Digital Marketing Fundamentals
  • Lead Generation and Customer Engagement
  • Sales and Business Development Basics
  • AI Tools for Productivity and Professional Growth

Benefits & Performance Incentives

This internship does not include an upfront stipend. However, interns will receive performance-based incentives for:

  • Successful enrollment of mentees into mentorship programs.
  • Sales of books, courses, and other learning products.

There is no limit on incentive earnings. The more value you create, the greater your earning potential.

What You'll Receive

  • Certificate of Completion
  • Practical industry exposure
  • Job Readiness Training
  • Mentorship from an experienced career mentor
  • Hands-on experience in marketing, sales, and career services
  • Opportunity to build a strong professional profile

Outstanding performers may be considered for future paid opportunities, subject to business requirements.

Make an Impact

If you are eager to learn, develop valuable professional skills, and make a meaningful impact on the careers of job seekers, we would love to hear from you.
